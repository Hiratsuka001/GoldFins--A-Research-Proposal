# References

Sources cited in the proposal and poster for **Detecting Fish Hunger Levels by Monitoring and Classifying Their Underwater Swimming Patterns**.

---

**[1]** Fagun, I. A., Rishan, S. T., Shipra, N. T., & Kunda, M. (2020). Present Status of Aquaculture and Socio-Economic Condition of Fish Farmers in a Rural Setting in Bangladesh. *Research in Agriculture, Livestock and Fisheries*, 7(2), 329–339. [https://doi.org/10.3329/ralf.v7i2.48876](https://doi.org/10.3329/ralf.v7i2.48876)
> Survey of Bangladeshi fish farmers' socio-economic conditions — supports the claim that high feed cost is one of the most commonly reported constraints farmers face.

**[2]** Hossain, M. A., Hossain, M. A., Haque, M. A., Begum, M. N., Akter, S., Noor, N. M., Abas, A., & Das, S. K. (2025). Evaluating Feeding Strategies to Improve Growth and Profitability in Carp Fattening. *Aquaculture and Fisheries* (in press).
> Backs up the ~60% feed-cost figure used throughout the proposal, in a Bangladeshi carp-farming context specifically.

**[3]** Måløy, H., Aamodt, A., & Misimi, E. (2019). A Spatio-Temporal Recurrent Network for Salmon Feeding Action Recognition from Underwater Videos in Aquaculture. *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture*, 167, 105087. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2019.105087](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2019.105087)
> Methodological precedent: uses a CNN + LSTM (spatio-temporal) network on underwater video to classify salmon feeding behavior — the closest existing approach to what we're proposing for hunger-level classification.

**[4]** Zhou, C., Xu, D., & Wang, Y. (2019). Evaluation of Fish Feeding Intensity in Aquaculture Using a Convolutional Neural Network and Machine Vision. *Aquaculture*, 507, 457–465. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aquaculture.2019.04.056](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aquaculture.2019.04.056)
> Shows CNN-based machine vision has already been used to estimate feeding intensity from video, supporting the feasibility of a camera-only approach.
